Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stravius Tech Pte. Ltd. ("Stravius Tech", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit straviustech.com (the "Site"), book a call, or contact us about our services.

By using the Site or contacting us, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Company: Stravius Tech Pte. Ltd., a private limited company registered in Singapore (UEN 202520316E)

Registered office: 60 Paya Lebar Road #07-54, Paya Lebar Square, Singapore 409051

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, work email, company, phone number and message.
  • Resource requests: your work email, if you ask us to send you a guide or template.
  • Booking a call: your name, email, the time you choose and any answers you give in the booking form.
  • Email, phone and WhatsApp: your contact details and the content of your messages.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, our analytics and advertising tools collect information such as pages visited, time on page, clicks and scrolling, the site or ad that referred you, browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Microsoft Clarity also records how you interact with pages (mouse movements, clicks and scrolling) so we can see where the Site is confusing.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange calls
  • To send you the resources you request
  • To follow up about the conversation you started with us
  • To understand how the Site is used and improve it
  • To measure whether our online ads lead to visits, bookings or enquiries

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We will not add you to a marketing list unless you request a resource or agree to it, and every marketing email includes a way to unsubscribe.

4. Service providers we use

We use the following providers to run the Site and our communications. They process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Tag Manager loads our analytics and advertising tags (Google privacy policy)
  • Google Analytics measures how the Site is used (Google LLC)
  • Google Ads measures whether our ads lead to visits, bookings or enquiries (Google LLC)
  • Microsoft Clarity shows how visitors use pages, including session recordings (Microsoft privacy statement)
  • Calendly runs the booking calendar on the Site (Calendly privacy notice)
  • FormSubmit delivers contact and resource-request form submissions to our email
  • WhatsApp carries messages if you contact us that way (WhatsApp privacy policy)

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies for three purposes:

  • Analytics (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity): to understand how visitors use the Site.
  • Advertising measurement (Google Ads): to see whether a visit, booking or enquiry came from one of our ads.
  • Booking (Calendly): the booking calendar may set its own cookies when it loads near the bottom of the page.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on and manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center. Blocking cookies does not stop you reading the Site or contacting us.

6. Transfers outside Singapore

Some of our providers store or process data outside Singapore, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on providers that are bound to protect personal data to a standard comparable to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

7. Payments

We do not take payments through the Site. Fees are agreed directly with clients and paid by bank transfer or another method we agree.

8. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, to meet legal, tax or accounting requirements, or to resolve disputes. After that we delete or anonymise it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data from unauthorised access, use, disclosure, alteration or loss. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Site and our services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Singapore (PDPA)

  • Ask for access to your personal data and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year
  • Ask us to correct personal data that is wrong or incomplete
  • Withdraw your consent to our collecting, using or disclosing your personal data

EU/EEA (GDPR)

  • Access, correct or delete your data, and receive a copy of it
  • Restrict or object to how we process it
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

California (CCPA/CPRA)

  • Know what personal data we collect and how we use it
  • Ask us to delete or correct it
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights

To use any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at sonia@straviustech.com. We will reply within 30 days.

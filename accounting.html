Questions accounting firms ask.

Can we trust AI with client data and documents?

An NDA and a data processing agreement come first. The automation runs inside your own accounts, I build with synthetic data and I keep client files out of public AI tools. It has to pass the tests we agree on before launch, and anything it isn't sure about goes to a person.

How is this different from a client portal, ChatGPT or offshore staff?

Portals collect files, but someone still has to chase the clients who don't upload. ChatGPT needs someone to paste files in, which is how client data ends up in public tools. Offshore staff give you more people to manage. This runs inside your own accounts, works alongside your portal and alerts you if something fails.

Does it work with QuickBooks, Xero and practice software like Karbon or TaxDome?

In most cases, yes. In the audit I confirm which of your tools it can connect to and how. If I can't connect something safely, you'll know before you pay anything.

Do we need anyone technical, and how much of our time will it take?

You don't need anyone technical. I build it, connect it to your accounts and set up the alerts. I need someone who knows your process in week 1 to map the workflow and agree the tests, and again in week 2 to review it. Your team then gets a walkthrough at handover.

Can it be live before busy season or tax season?

Plan on about four weeks. The audit report arrives within three business days and a sprint takes three weeks from kickoff, so book at least four weeks before you need it live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Alerts tell us before your clients notice. Anything that stops passing the agreed tests is fixed at no charge for 45 days after launch. After that, Ongoing Support covers it.

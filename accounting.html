  • Reply from a Different Email Address
  • Two Clients with the Same Name
  • Partial Upload
  • "I already sent it"

Questions accounting firms ask.

Does it work with QuickBooks, Xero or our practice software?

In most cases, yes. In the audit I confirm which of your tools it can connect to and how. If I can't connect something safely, you'll know before you pay anything.

We already use a client portal. Is this still useful?

Often. Portals collect files, but someone still has to chase the clients who don't upload. The automation can work alongside your portal's reminders or fill the gaps around them, and the audit will show whether it's worth it for your firm.

Do we need anyone technical on our side?

No. I build it, connect it to your accounts and set up the alerts. Your team needs to know your process, not the technology, and gets a walkthrough at handover.

Will clients find automated reminders annoying?

Each reminder lists only what that client still owes, and the reminders stop as soon as they've sent it. We agree the wording and schedule with you before anything goes live.

Is our client data safe?

An NDA and a data processing agreement come first. The automation runs inside your own accounts, I build with synthetic data, and I keep client files out of public AI tools.

Can it be ready before busy season?

The audit report arrives within three business days, and a sprint takes three weeks from kickoff. Book at least five weeks before you need it live.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Alerts tell us before your clients notice. Anything that stops passing the agreed tests is fixed at no charge for 45 days after launch. After that, Ongoing Support covers it.

Will it replace our staff?

No. It takes the chasing and retyping off your seniors' desks, so they spend busy season on client work. Anything the automation isn't sure about still goes to a person.

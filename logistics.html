Questions logistics teams ask.

Does it work with our TMS such as CargoWise, Magaya or Descartes?

Most TMS platforms accept data through an integration, an import file or an email inbox. In the audit, I check which of these yours supports. If I can't connect it safely, you'll know before you pay anything.

Do we need anyone technical on our side?

No, you do not. I build it, connect it to your systems and set up the alerts. Your team needs to know your process (not the technology) and gets a walkthrough at handover.

What if it misreads a document?

If the automation isn't confident about a field, it flags the document for a person to check. We include those cases in the agreed tests.

Is our customer and shipment data safe?

We sign an NDA and a data processing agreement before I touch anything. The automation runs inside your own accounts. I build with synthetic data and real customer data is only used after the tests pass and you've signed off.

How long does it take to go live?

The audit report arrives within three business days and the sprint takes three weeks from kickoff. Plan on about five weeks from the first call to a live automation.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Alerts tell us before your customers notice. Anything that stops passing the agreed tests is fixed at no charge for 45 days after launch. After that, ongoing support covers it.

Will it replace our operations team?

No. It takes the retyping and chasing off their desks so the same team can handle more shipments. Anything the automation isn't sure about still goes to a person.

We're in the US. How does the time difference work?

I'm in Singapore so I work on problems you report in your evening during my day and the fix is often in place by the time you're back.
